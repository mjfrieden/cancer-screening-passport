Delete Your Account

Cancer Prevention Passport is operated by White Cloud Medical, LLC.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Cancer Prevention Passport.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Open account data controls and choose the permanent deletion action.
  4. Complete Google reauthentication and confirm deletion.

This deletes the Cancer Prevention Passport app identity and user-owned app records. It does not delete the underlying Google account.

Request deletion without app access

Email support@whitecloudmedical.com from the email address associated with the account. White Cloud Medical will verify the request before deleting account data.

Do not include health information, passwords, exports, or identity documents in email.

Data deleted or retained

The deletion process removes the user profile, screening records, survivorship records, consent records, and Firebase app identity associated with the account. Limited security, legal, or incident-response records may be retained only when required by an approved retention obligation. Deletion requests and exceptions are documented without placing health information in support messages.
