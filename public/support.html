Support

Operated by White Cloud Medical, LLC. Last updated June 30, 2026.

Cancer Prevention Passport is a production health education and record-organizing service from White Cloud Medical, LLC. Contact support@whitecloudmedical.com for account, privacy, or support requests. Users can also follow the account-deletion instructions. Public product feedback may be filed through GitHub issues after removing personal and health information.

Account and Data Requests

Signed-in users can export a local JSON copy of app data and delete app data/account records from the Profile tab.

Security or Privacy Concerns

Do not post protected health information, sensitive personal details, private tokens, or security concerns in public GitHub issues. Email support@whitecloudmedical.com for private privacy or health-data concerns. Security researchers may also use GitHub Security Advisories.

Public Product Feedback

Use the repository's bug report or product feedback templates only for non-sensitive product, technical, UX, or clinical-language feedback.

Medical Questions

This app does not provide medical advice. Contact a licensed clinician for screening, symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, or survivorship care questions.
