Cancer Prevention Passport is a patient-held organizer. It is not medical advice, a medical device, diagnostic tool, treatment recommendation system, or emergency service.

Guideline-Inspired Reminders

Recommendations and dates are generated from information entered into the app and simplified guideline-inspired logic. They may not reflect your complete medical history, risk factors, symptoms, genetic background, clinician judgment, local practice standards, or the latest guideline changes.

Clinician Review Required

Always review screening intervals, abnormal results, survivorship surveillance, symptoms, medications, and family history with a licensed clinician. Do not delay or change care based only on this app.

Emergency Care

If you believe you may have a medical emergency, call emergency services or seek immediate medical attention.
