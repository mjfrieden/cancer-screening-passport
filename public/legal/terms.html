The app does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure disease. It does not replace professional medical judgment. Always consult qualified clinicians before making healthcare decisions.

This software is under active development. Features, recommendations, exports, and availability may change. Do not rely on the beta as the sole source of truth for medical care.

Exports

PDF, QR, and FHIR-style JSON exports are patient-held summaries generated from entered data. They may be incomplete or inaccurate if source information is incomplete or inaccurate.
