Privacy Policy

Beta template. Last updated June 20, 2026.

Cancer Prevention Passport helps signed-in users organize screening history, profile information, survivorship context, and patient-held exports. This policy describes the intended beta data practices and should be reviewed before any public clinical launch.

Information We Collect

The app may collect account identifiers from Firebase Authentication and health-related information that you enter, including demographic profile details, smoking history, cancer history, survivorship plan details, screening events, and screening results.

How We Use Information

Information is used to show your profile, save your screening history, generate guideline-inspired reminders, and create patient-held PDF or FHIR-style exports for your review with clinicians.

Sharing

The app does not sell personal information. You control when to download or show exports. Any QR, PDF, or JSON export may contain health information and should be shared carefully.

Storage and Security

The beta uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore. Production use with protected health information requires a dedicated production environment, access controls, audit practices, vendor review, and any required business associate agreements.

Your Choices

Signed-in users can export a local JSON copy of app data and delete app data/account records from the Profile tab. Production support processes should be finalized before public launch.

Medical Disclaimer

The app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Confirm all screening decisions with licensed clinicians.

Contact

See the support page. Add a monitored production support email before public release.
