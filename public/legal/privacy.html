Privacy Policy

Effective June 28, 2026.

White Cloud Medical, LLC operates Cancer Prevention Passport, a health education and record-organizing service. This policy explains the current public-beta data practices. It is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review before unrestricted production use with real health information.

Information We Collect

The app may collect account identifiers from Firebase Authentication and health-related information that you enter, including demographic profile details, smoking history, cancer history, survivorship plan details, screening events, and screening results.

How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate your account, display your profile, save screening history, generate educational guideline-inspired reminders, create patient-held exports, protect the service, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support, privacy, or security requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use health information for targeted advertising. We may disclose information to service providers that host or secure the service, when you direct an export, when required by law, or to protect users and the service. Any QR, PDF, or JSON export may contain health information and should be shared carefully.

Service Providers

The beta uses Google Firebase Authentication and Firestore for sign-in and app-record storage, respectively, and Cloudflare Pages for static web delivery. Google may receive account and authentication information, and Firestore stores app records. Cloudflare processes web requests and associated technical data. Their independent privacy terms also apply.

Storage and Security

We use access-control rules and encrypted network connections, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Do not enter protected health information or highly sensitive details during the public beta. Production use with protected health information requires a dedicated production environment, vendor review, access controls, audit and incident-response practices, and any required business associate agreements.

HIPAA and Consumer Health Information

The service is not represented as HIPAA-compliant or HIPAA-ready. HIPAA coverage depends on who offers the service and why. Consumer health information outside HIPAA may still be protected by other laws, including the Federal Trade Commission Act and the Health Breach Notification Rule.

Your Choices

Signed-in users can export a local JSON copy of app data and delete app data and the Firebase app identity from the Profile tab. The underlying Google account is not deleted. You may also stop using the service. Some limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or documented incident response.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and currently requires account holders to be at least 18. Do not create an account for a child or enter a child's information during beta.

Data Breaches

White Cloud Medical, LLC will evaluate suspected unauthorized access or disclosure and provide notices required by applicable law. The FTC Health Breach Notification Rule may apply to consumer health applications that are not covered by HIPAA.

Medical Disclaimer

The app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Confirm all screening decisions with licensed clinicians.

Contact

See the support page for current public and private contact channels. A monitored company support address and named privacy/incident-response owner remain required before unrestricted production use.
