Privacy Policy

Effective June 30, 2026.

White Cloud Medical, LLC operates Cancer Prevention Passport as a production health education and record-organizing service. This policy explains the current production data practices. It is not a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review before use with real health information.

Information We Collect

The app may collect account identifiers from Firebase Authentication and health-related information that you enter, including demographic profile details, smoking history, cancer history, survivorship plan details, screening events, and screening results.

How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate your account, display your profile, save screening history, generate educational guideline-inspired reminders, create patient-held exports, protect the service, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support, privacy, or security requests.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use health information for targeted advertising. We may disclose information to service providers that host or secure the service, when you direct an export, when required by law, or to protect users and the service. Any QR, PDF, or JSON export may contain health information and should be shared carefully.

Service Providers

The service uses Google Identity Platform and Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Firestore for app-record storage, and Google App Engine Standard for web delivery. Google may process account, authentication, app-record, security, and technical request information to provide these services. Their independent privacy terms also apply.

Storage and Security

We use access-control rules and encrypted network connections, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Use the service only in ways permitted by your clinician, employer, or other applicable policies. Production use with protected health information relies on the current production environment, vendor review, access controls, audit and incident-response practices, and any required business associate agreements.

HIPAA and Consumer Health Information

The service is not represented as HIPAA-compliant or HIPAA-ready. HIPAA coverage depends on who offers the service and why. Consumer health information outside HIPAA may still be protected by other laws, including the Federal Trade Commission Act and the Health Breach Notification Rule.

Your Choices

Signed-in users can export a local JSON copy of app data and delete app data and the Firebase app identity from the Profile tab. The underlying Google account is not deleted. Users who cannot access the app may follow the public account-deletion instructions. Some limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or documented incident response.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and currently requires account holders to be at least 18. Do not create an account for a child or enter a child's information.

Data Breaches

White Cloud Medical, LLC will evaluate suspected unauthorized access or disclosure and provide notices required by applicable law. The FTC Health Breach Notification Rule may apply to consumer health applications that are not covered by HIPAA.

Medical Disclaimer

The app is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. Confirm all screening decisions with licensed clinicians.

Contact

Contact White Cloud Medical, LLC at support@whitecloudmedical.com for support, privacy, security, access, export, correction, or deletion requests. See the support page for additional channels.
