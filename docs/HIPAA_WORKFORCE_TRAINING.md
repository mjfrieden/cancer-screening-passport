## Required Review

Each workforce member must review and understand:

1. Real PHI may be entered only after the production activation gate is
   formally approved.
2. PHI must not be placed in GitHub, public issues, support email, source code,
   logs, resource names, screenshots, or test fixtures.
3. Use the minimum necessary information for support and operations.
4. Never share passwords, verification codes, session tokens, exports, or
   service credentials.
5. Report suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, deletion, or
   unavailability immediately.
6. Follow `docs/HEALTH_DATA_INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`; preserve evidence and do not
   improvise notifications.
7. Verify account deletion requests before acting and never request PHI by
   email.
8. Review administrator access quarterly and remove unnecessary access
   promptly.
9. Confirm recommendations with clinicians; the application is educational
   and is not emergency care.
10. Escalate vendor, architecture, privacy-policy, and clinical-content changes
    before release.
